## The Fear

People ask: "Is coding dead?" What they really mean: "Is memorizing syntax still valuable?"

## The Answer

**Syntax was never the real skill.** AI exposed this brutally.

## What Still Matters

AI can generate code. But it cannot:
- Own architecture decisions
- Understand long-term tradeoffs
- Debug invisible assumptions
- Design systems for scale and failure

## The New Engineer Split

**Group A**: Copies AI output, can't explain why it works, breaks under complexity.

**Group B**: Uses AI as a multiplier, reads code critically, designs before generating.

**Only one group survives long-term.**

## Takeaway

AI didn't replace engineers. **It removed the hiding place.**

---

*You're not meant to consume AI content endlessly, chase every new tool, or memorize everything. You're meant to think deeply, build deliberately, and question assumptions.*
